Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Start 'Em is a free fantasy football game. This page explains what we collect when you play and what we do with it. The short version: we keep only what the game needs, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google or an email code, we receive your email address (and, with Google, your basic profile). We use it only to log you in.
  • Your username, shown on leaderboards, groups and challenges.
  • Game data: your picks, scores, streaks, ranked rating, best lineup, and the groups you create or join.
  • Reminder subscription. If you turn on daily reminders, your browser gives us a push address so we can send one notification a day. Turn it off any time in your profile.
  • On your device, the site saves a few small settings (like whether you've seen the how-to-play screen) in your browser's local storage.

Casual mode works without an account. Those games aren't linked to you and are cleared out regularly.

What's public

Your username, scores and lineups appear on leaderboards, in groups you join, and on challenge links and share cards you create. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

Who handles the data

The game runs on Supabase (login and database) and GitHub Pages (hosting). Login codes are emailed through Brevo. We don't use ads or tracking analytics, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything tied to it, open an issue at github.com/msilverberg1401/start-em/issues asking to be removed (no personal details needed in the issue; we'll reach out to confirm). Deleting an account removes its games, results, stats, groups and reminder subscription.

Kids

Start 'Em isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
